The Artist

One artist. Many ways of telling stories.

I'm an actor, poet, playwright, producer, teaching artist, style architect, and creative consultant. My practice moves between performance, writing, education, fashion, and collaborative experiences.

With a BFA in Theatre from Cornish College of the Arts and over a decade of collaboration with Theatre Battery, I bring theatrical training and a storyteller's perspective to each space I enter.

I believe that stories can be spoken, staged, taught, worn, and experienced—and that creative work grows stronger when these expressions meet.

My Philosophy

“Your outside is the physical architecture of your inside.”

Whether I'm interpreting a character, shaping a production, helping young learners find their voices, or exploring personal style, I return to the relationship between identity and expression.

Teaching Artistry

Learning through sound, story & movement

Teaching is a central part of my creative practice—not an extension of it.

As an Artist-in-Residence with Shunpike at The Studio @ 2+U in downtown Seattle, I'm developing The Sound of Essentials Reading Room, a free, family-centered experience that connects original music, a companion storybook, early literacy, imaginative play, and theatre movement.

My approach also draws from acting coaching, scene study, Alexander Technique, breathwork, and embodied performance.
